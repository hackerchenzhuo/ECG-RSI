01 — THE IDEA

Turn a missing capability into a checkable training example.

ECG interpretation connects waveform evidence to measurements and clinical conclusions. The challenge is to construct specific ECG changes, consistent questions, and explanations grounded in the resulting signal.

ECG-RSI makes data construction responsive to the learner. Forward synthesis starts with an observed ECG. Reverse synthesis starts with a desired feature contrast—including an eligible distractor from a training question—and edits the waveform to realize that contrast before regenerating supervision.

Construct the evidence.
Verify the example. Learn from the feedback.
Read the abstract

Electrocardiogram (ECG) models need supervision that targets evolving weaknesses and remains faithful to waveform evidence. We introduce ECG-RSI, a recursive self-improvement framework combining verified waveform synthesis with feedback-guided intervention search. Signal remeasurement verifies waveform edits; construction errors guide generator repair, while learner errors motivate interventions compared on a separate selection set. Across seven benchmarks, three rounds improve Qwen3.5-35B-A3B by 21.47 percentage points in mean QA accuracy and 9.77 points in mean classification F1. Across the retained trajectory, the preferred supervision shifts from direct reinforcement to confusion-focused contrasts. Continued feedback correction yields diminishing external gains. RSI should therefore separate error diagnosis from intervention selection, renewing how it teaches as the learner evolves and assessing progress through external transfer.

02 — THE KEY INSIGHT

What changes across RSI rounds?

Error diagnosis and intervention selection are distinct.

Feedback identifies gaps and motivates candidate interventions; training and candidate comparison determine which update is retained.

Reselect interventions as learner needs evolve.

At each round, ECG-RSI explores candidate branches and selects the best qualifying update on a separate selection set, adapting interventions to the learner’s evolving needs.

Local correction has diminishing external returns.

Feedback error rates fall by 5.80, 5.17, and 4.10 pp, while external S7 gains shrink to 13.07, 3.01, and 0.38 pp. Later rounds warrant checks of transfer and regression.

03 — THE METHOD

One data engine. Two connected feedback loops.

SIGNAL → INSTRUCTION

Forward synthesis

Extract evidence from a real training-side waveform and generate aligned questions, answers, and explanations.

TARGET → SIGNAL → INSTRUCTION

Reverse synthesis

Translate a target into constrained waveform edits, remeasure the result, and construct supervision from achieved evidence.

01

Diagnose

Identify errors by capability, difficulty, and evidence representation.

02

Construct & verify

Synthesize candidates and repair rejected operators and prompts.

03

Train & compare

Train candidate branches from a shared parent with historical replay.

04

Select & repeat

Promote the winner and carry its accepted new questions forward.

Each winning round adds 10,000 previously unused accepted questions to the cumulative training set, alongside replay of all previous training data.

Verify the signal, then construct supervision.

Apply a constrained edit, remeasure its target and protected features, and build the question and answer from the resulting evidence. Construction failures guide repairs to waveform operators, retrieval rules, and question-generation prompts.

BRANCH A

Direct reinforcement

Target weak categories and the question types exposing the deficit.

BRANCH B

Confusion contrast

Teach the ECG evidence that distinguishes recurrently confused diagnoses.

BRANCH C

Difficulty & task curriculum

Adjust the mixture of question types and learner-relative difficulty.

Feedback proposes candidates from the same parent. A separate selection set determines which checkpoint and new data continue to the next round. External benchmarks assess transfer.

06 — ANALYSIS

Both the synthesis route and the selection rule matter.

Starting from the same checkpoint (S7 = 43.22), each configuration adds 10,000 questions for continued supervised fine-tuning.

Repair improves synthesis efficiency.

The full method requires 16,193 candidates for 10,000 accepted questions. Freezing operator updates requires 36,723; freezing skill updates requires 22,398.

Clinical review checks more than targets.

Two human physicians independently review 240 examples. Target attainment is 0.82, non-target preservation 0.79, and answer accuracy 0.88. Inter-rater Cohen’s κ is 0.78.

Uniform allocation and chain updates improve in earlier rounds but decline at R3. The tree trajectory continues to improve. Together with the construction ablation, these results show that both the composition of new data and the strategy used to retain updates affect subsequent performance.
